Heaven is Round, Earth is Square

天圆地方

A Player-Built Virtual Civilization

🎮 Enter Game (Beta) 📖 View Wiki

Heaven is Round, Earth is Square: HRES is a free, open, lightweight online game. In this vast, persistent pixel world there are no NPCs, no instances, no preset story—everything is created by real players. Here you become a person living in a virtual world, taking part in a real economy and collective society: gathering, running industries, building towns, founding nations—every step driven by player collaboration.

⚠️ Beta launched on January 16, 2026.

Core Systems

Core mechanics and gameplay

🌍 World & Time

A vast, continuous world with varied terrain. The game uses a unique calendar: the Tianyuan Era, with the day as the basic unit for daily settlement.

⛏️ Resources & Gathering

Trees, stone, iron ore, copper ore and other resources are distributed across the world. Players spend stamina to gather and obtain raw materials for development.

🏭 Industry & Production

Production industries consume materials to produce goods; service industries provide services for income. Players can work at others’ industries for pay.

🏗️ Building System

Buildings fall into three categories: industry, public, and infrastructure—supporting production, town management, and connectivity.

🏘️ Town System

Players can create and develop towns, the core unit of settlement. Town lords manage towns and can assign titles and roles to residents.

👑 Nation System

One or more towns can form a nation; government form is decided by players. Institutions and rules emerge through player interaction and agreement.

🗺️ Territory System

The world is divided into chunks; each chunk is the unit for territory, building, and resources. Towns expand by building town centers and outposts.
